---
title: Manage billing and credits
description: Fund managed compute, understand charges, and configure automatic top ups.
---

Metal uses prepaid organization credits for managed capacity. Provider usage is deducted at cost. The platform fee applies when credits are purchased.

## Purchase credits

The purchase fee is 5.5 percent of the credit amount with a USD 0.80 minimum.

```bash
openmetal billing show --organization <organization-id>
openmetal billing quote --organization <organization-id> --amount-usd 20.00
```

The dashboard confirms the final amount before opening Stripe Checkout. A success redirect never changes the balance by itself. Signed Stripe webhooks are the source of truth.

## Automatic top ups

Owners and admins can choose a balance threshold, refill amount, and monthly cap after saving a payment method.

A declined payment or required customer action pauses the policy instead of retrying indefinitely. Update the payment method in the dashboard to recover.

## Spend behavior

Managed sandbox creation requires a balance above zero. Usage is charged when durable provider cost evidence arrives, so delayed metering can temporarily produce a negative balance.

When a durable charge leaves the balance at or below zero and no automatic top up can run, Metal schedules managed sandboxes to stop.

## BYOK

BYOK capacity is billed by the provider and does not consume Metal credits. A single explicitly selected BYOK provider without fallback can create capacity without a positive managed balance.

<Callout type="info" title="Keep money exact">
  Metal stores amounts as integer micro USD. Never calculate balances or fees with binary floating
  point.
</Callout>
